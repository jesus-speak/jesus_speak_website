Help & Support

Have questions about reading scriptures, watching biblical video episodes, taking quizzes, or creating verse posters? Find quick answers below or reach out to us.

How do I switch the Bible and video audio language?

You can switch languages between English, Malayalam, and Hindi anytime under Profile > App Language, or directly from the More Options menu while watching video reels.

Can I download videos or stories for offline viewing?

Offline downloads are supported in premium plans. You can also tap the Heart icon on any video reel to save it to your Liked Episodes, or tap the Bookmark icon on any scripture verse. Access all your saved favorites anytime under Profile > Bookmarks and Liked Episodes.

What is the Verse Poster feature and how do I use it?

The Verse Poster feature lets you create and share beautiful scripture cards. While reading any Bible chapter or viewing the daily verse, tap the Share icon to open the Verse Poster sheet. You can choose from scenic background templates, highlight keywords, save high-resolution posters to your device gallery, or share directly to social media.

How do Episode Quizzes work?

After watching biblical video stories, test your understanding by taking multiple-choice episode quizzes. You can review your scores, passed quizzes, and pending quizzes under Profile > Episode Quizzes.

How do Bible Reading Plans work?

You can join featured reading plans from the Home screen or create a custom plan tailored to your pace. The app automatically tracks your completed chapters and daily reading streaks.
